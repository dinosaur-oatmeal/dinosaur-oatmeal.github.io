USDA ARS / JULY 2025 – JULY 2026 / PART-TIME

One-Month-Ahead HPAI Forecasting

Developed a national county-level forecasting framework with strict temporal cutoffs and automated monthly backtesting. Building on my prior internship's pseudo-forecasting work, this framework replaced unavailable future covariates with estimates generated from historical data.

Evaluated 40 model configurations across a 24-month rolling-origin backtest. Translated raw forecast outputs into biosurveillance tiers in an interactive dashboard.

SCALE: 74,616 county-month forecasts
RESULT: 73% balanced accuracy / 74% recall
PREVALENCE: 0.8% outbreak-positive
USDA ARS / MAY – JULY 2025 / INTERNSHIP

HPAI Risk Classification & Pseudo-Forecasting

Developed national county-month risk models using environmental, agricultural, geospatial, and migratory-bird data from 2022–2024. Rare-event classification achieved 75%+ balanced accuracy on data with approximately 1% outbreak-positive records.

Pseudo-forecasting evaluated performance under temporal shift using future climate covariates. This research aided in determining if early-warning signal could be retained for future forward-looking forecasting models.

Presented findings to USDA ARS scientists, the Chief Scientist, and National Program Leaders. This research received the Judge's Choice Award at UTA College of Engineering Innovation Day in 2026.
